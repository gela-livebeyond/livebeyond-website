06 // A little more clarity

Good questions. Straight answers.

The first conversation should not have to start with “What do you actually do?”

Ask us something else
What is LiveBeyond, in one sentence?+

LiveBeyond helps organisations apply longevity science through scientific advisory, research-to-market support and BeyondClock biological-age technology.

How is LiveBeyond different from BeyondClock?+

LiveBeyond is the company and broader scientific-advisory offering. BeyondClock is its biological-age platform. You can discuss an advisory or partnership project without adopting the platform.

Do we need specialised genetic testing?+

BeyondClock is based on routine clinical biomarkers, not genomic sequencing. The full input set also includes physical measurements and questionnaire responses. Your existing panel may cover much of what is required; ask the team to check compatibility before assuming it is complete.

Does a biological-age score diagnose disease?+

No. The supplied sample report describes the software as intended for general wellbeing, not for medical purposes. Its outputs should not be treated as a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a prediction of lifespan. A change in the model’s estimate does not by itself demonstrate a health benefit.

What does an engagement cost?+

Request a scope and quote based on your use case. For BeyondClock, discuss panel requirements, report volumes and onboarding. For advisory work, specify the question, expected output and timing. Ask the team for a quote and any applicable minimum commitment.

Can I book a personal health assessment here?+

This page is for organisations exploring services or partnerships. It does not book medical appointments or accept personal laboratory reports. Individuals should speak with their healthcare provider about appropriate assessment.

About this concept


This is an independent, proposed rewrite and design prepared for discussion on 16 October 2026. It is not an authorised publication by LiveBeyond. The descriptive offer packaging, first-project examples, contact flow and working process are proposals for the team to approve—not claims that fixed packages already exist.


Positioning: LiveBeyond remains the umbrella business. Visitor needs come first; BeyondClock, BeyondImpact and BeyondInsight support the offers rather than force visitors to learn an internal brand hierarchy. The latter two names are retained from the preceding website review and require confirmation.

Decisions required before going live


    

  • Commercial scope: Confirm which advisory engagements are actively sold, the deliverables, expert availability, pricing approach and partnership criteria. Confirm Brian and Julie’s preferred biographies and all product/brand relationships.
    • 

  • Product and claims: The brochures use more expansive clinical language than the sample report’s non-medical intended-use statement. That discrepancy has not been resolved. This concept follows the report’s narrower description. The company must approve jurisdiction-specific intended use, claims and required disclosures before launch.
    • 

  • Operations: Verify required inputs, missing-marker handling, upload workflow, turnaround, access, data residency and contractual privacy arrangements. API integration, universal panel compatibility, response-time promises and clinical-outcome guarantees have deliberately not been invented.
    • 

  • Rights: Secure permission for the portraits, sample values and references before publishing. Team photographs are genuine supplied assets; no new likenesses were generated. Research collaborators are not represented as full-time employees.
    • 

  • Lead handling: Replace the local email-draft builder with an approved form/CRM or booking system only after the destination, consent and privacy terms are agreed. Do not enable patient-file uploads on a marketing enquiry form.
    • 

  • Publication: Replace this concept banner and notes after approval; add approved privacy/legal pages, live-domain metadata and a social-sharing image; remove any noindex directive only for the authorised public launch.

02 // BeyondClock sample

Inside a BeyondClock report









An adapted preview using selected values from the supplied sample. This is a report explanation, not a live calculation or medical assessment.













One estimate. In context.




Chronological age
57.5 yrs


Estimated biological age
60.6 yrs




The sample estimate is 3.1 years above chronological age. It summarises biomarker-based signals through the model. It does not mean every organ is 60.6 years old or say how long this person will live.


A useful next question is not simply “Is the number high or low?” It is “Which patterns contributed to it, and how should those be interpreted in context?”










Look beneath the headline number.


The model groups related inputs into principal components (“PCs”). These selected rows show their reported contributions to the estimate.




PC1F
+5.4 years


PC24F
+2.7 years


PC23F
+2.5 years


PC6F
−2.8 years




These are only selected contributors. They do not add up to the overall age difference, and they are not stand-alone treatment targets.










Know what the report cannot tell you.


The sample report states that the software is intended for general wellbeing, not for medical purposes. Its output is not medical advice.


Its biomarker simulations are mathematical “what happens to the model?” exercises—not proven interventions or recommendations to change a laboratory value.


The report’s anatomical headings organise disease- and risk-domain results. The sample explicitly states that these are not organ-level scores or diagnoses.


A lower biological-age estimate is not, by itself, proof of slower aging, disease prevention, added healthy years or treatment effectiveness.
